📲 Install 2KT Chating App

2KT Chating App
Works standalone on any phone, tablet or PC

Installing on iPhone & iPad (iOS):

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button ⬆️ (at bottom or top of screen).
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" ➕.
  3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
  4. 2KT Chating will now open as a full-screen native app directly from your home screen!

Installing on Android, Windows, Mac or Linux:

  1. Click or tap "Install App" in the browser prompt or header.
  2. On Android Chrome: Tap the three dots (⋮) → tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".
  3. On Chrome/Edge PC: Click the Install icon (computer with down arrow) on the right side of the address bar.
  4. Enjoy zero browser bars, instant launch from your desktop or app drawer!
